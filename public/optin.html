Plainly, before you start.

Palmistry cannot predict your future. There is no evidence that it can, and Benjamin will never give you a date, a name, or a promise about what is going to happen.

What it can do is give you language. Most people carry patterns they have felt for years and never had said back to them plainly. That is the thing that is actually useful here, and that is the part people tend to sit with afterward.

Read it the way you would read a good letter about yourself. Keep what fits. Leave what does not.

Questions people ask first.

Is this really free?

The full reading of all four lines is free, with no card and no account. Afterward you can choose to go deeper into one area of your life, and that part is paid. You will see the price before anything else happens.

What happens to my photo?

It is used to write your reading and nothing else. It is not sold, shared, or published anywhere.

How is this different from a horoscope?

A horoscope is written once for millions of people born in the same month. This is written from your hand, and it commits to specific things that could be wrong about you rather than safe things that are true about everyone.

Is a person reading my palm?

No. Benjamin is an AI reader, and we say so plainly rather than pretending otherwise. The reading is written fresh from your photo, not pulled from a template.

What if my photo is not good enough?

Benjamin will tell you what to fix and let you try again rather than guessing at lines he cannot see.
